    Getting started

    1. Load a BIOS dumped from your own console: o2rom.bin for a G7000 or Odyssey², g7400.bin for a G7400.
    2. Insert a cartridge image (.bin, .rom or a .zip containing one).
    3. When the screen says SELECT GAME, press a number key. On a controller, Start is 1 and Select is 2.

    Joystick controls

    • Gamepad: d-pad or left stick moves, A, B, X or Y fires.
    • Keyboard, player 1: arrow keys, fire with Space or Right Shift.
    • Keyboard, player 2: Numpad 8 4 6 2, fire with Numpad 0.
    • Phones and tablets: on-screen stick and fire button. Turn the phone sideways, or tap Play mode, to fill the screen.

    About the hardware

    Emulates the 8048 CPU with bank-switched cartridges up to 16 KB, the 8244/8245 video and sound chip with sprite and character collisions, and the EF9340/41 text chip of the G7400 used by Videopac+ games. Games marked for the Odyssey² usually expect NTSC timing.
